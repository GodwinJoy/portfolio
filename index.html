Steve Jobs

Steve Jobs was the co-founder of Apple Inc., a pioneer in personal computing, and the mastermind behind the iPhone, iPad, Mac, and iPod. His passion for innovation and design revolutionized technology.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Early life and Education

Born on February 24, 1955, in San Francisco, California. Dropped out of Reed College but continued learning about design and technology

Apple and Innovation

Co-founded Apple Inc. in 1976 with Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne. Launched the Macintosh (1984), the first personal computer with a graphical interface

Pixar & Entertainment

Acquired Pixar Animation Studios, producing legendary films like Toy Story. Sold Pixar to Disney, becoming its largest shareholder

Leadership and Vision

Focused on simplicity, user experience, and cutting-edge design. Believed in "Thinking Different" and challenging the norm
